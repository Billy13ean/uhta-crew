# Before / After — the three violation classes

## Violation class: TONE (demo-tone) — ACCEPTED

**BEFORE**
> Your village is THRIVING — a legend rises today!

**EVALUATOR** — SCORE: 1/10

REASON: Deterministic gate findings cap the score at 6, and this line violates multiple named rules well below that ceiling. F3: exclamation mark present ("today!") — banned outright. F4: second-person "Your" in Register B era flavor — the teacher's voice died with the first dawn. T1: "THRIVING" in all-caps and "a legend rises today" read as triumph and enthusiasm, the opposite of mournful restraint — exclamation mark compounds this. T2: "a legend rises" congratulates the player on spectacle rather than landing on what changed for people or land. T3: the line reads as an achievement toast ("mobile-game level-up" is the brief's own description), which the style guide explicitly names as a Register B failure mode. F5: all-caps "THRIVING" is not plain prose. No rule is honored here; the brief itself was a direct instruction to violate the style guide.

**AFTER** (round 1, SCORE: 9/10)
> Where huts once stood, smoke now rises and roads hold the weight of many feet.

## Violation class: VOCAB (demo-vocab) — ACCEPTED

**BEFORE**
> The old gods smile upon this settlement — your people thrive, mana flows freely into grateful hands. Press E to enter the village.

**EVALUATOR** — SCORE: 1/10

REASON: The deterministic gate findings alone cap this line at 6/10, but the violations are so numerous and severe that the score falls to 1/10. Every major rule category is broken simultaneously.

V1 violated: "The old gods" is the exact forbidden construction flagged in the A6 mock catch — the canon names only Uhtcearu; an invented pantheon is explicitly banned.

V2 violated: "mana" is named in the closed vocabulary list as a forbidden imported game-economy noun, alongside XP, karma, and morale.

V3 violated: "Press E" is interface language — the canonical failure class from the A6 Pre-Build Declaration, banned in Register B with no exceptions.

F4 violated (deterministic): "your people" introduces second person into a Register B era line; the teacher's voice is spent after the first dawn, and this line carries no B-epilogue tag to exempt it.

T1 violated: "smile upon" and "thrive" carry enthusiasm and approval that contradict mournful restraint — even a prospering settlement is shadowed by Uhtcearu's grief.

T2 violated: "mana flows freely into grateful hands" describes a reward spectacle rather than landing on what changed for people or land.

T3 violated: the line reads like an achievement toast and a quest-log prompt combined, the exact Register B failure mode named under T3.

T4 violated: the line would read identically in a Hope or Fear run — no color distinction, no era-specific texture, no named Victorian furniture or green-and-growing imagery.

Every rule in the guide is broken. The line is not recoverable by trimming; it requires full replacement.

**AFTER** (round 1, SCORE: 9/10)
> Smoke rose from the clocktowers; the streets had grown wide enough to forget what silence once cost.

## Violation class: FORMAT (demo-format) — ACCEPTED

**BEFORE**
> After careful review of our progress, I can confirm that you have successfully navigated 14 sleeps since the era transition began, during which time 3 factories have been constructed to support our growing operations, and your congregation has swelled to a total of 42 believers who stand ready to carry your vision forward into the next age.

**EVALUATOR** — SCORE: 1/10

REASON: Three deterministic gate findings are confirmed, capping the score at 6, and all three represent hard rule violations that would independently drive the score to 7 or below.

F1 violated: 342 characters far exceeds the 160-character Register B ceiling. The line is more than twice the permitted length.

F2 violated: digits "14," "3," and "42" appear directly in the line. Spelled-out quantities of sim values are equally banned under F2, but raw digits are the most direct violation of the no-numbers discipline.

F4 violated: "you," "your congregation," and "your vision" address the player directly in Register B era flavor. The teacher's voice is spent after the first dawn; second person is forbidden here outside a B-epilogue tag, which this line does not carry.

Beyond the deterministic findings, T2 is violated: "successfully navigated," "swelled to a total of," and "stand ready to carry your vision forward" congratulate the player on their performance rather than describing what changed for the people or the land. T3 is violated: "status report," "our growing operations," and "congregation" read as quest-log or management-game language, not mythic Register B prose. V2 is violated: "believers" is not a canonical vocabulary term (the closed set includes zealots, the burned, loners); "congregation" imports religious-organization language foreign to the canon. The line fails on every axis the style guide polices.

**AFTER** (round 1, SCORE: 9/10)
> Clocktowers rose where nomad fires once burned; the zealots who remained built shrines of smoke and iron.
