## Open tuning lever(s)

**Cooldown vs duration trade-off for spawn count.** B (duration 2, cooldown 2) held median 3 fronts while A (duration 3, cooldown 3) dropped to 2.5 — the sweep is duration ∈ {2, 2.5, 3} at fixed cooldown 2 to find the exposure-reduction threshold that preserves spawn count ≥ 3. The Designer's question 1 ("can straggler wear be brought below step-cap saturation") remains unanswered — all variants saturate the step cap at −1/tick during fronts (per Red-Teamer A2 arithmetic), and no probe measured per-tick grey rate directly.

**Radius reduction effect on spatial coverage.** C (radius 5) matched A's spawn count and exposure exactly despite the tighter radius — no measurable difference in aggregate metrics. The "sharper camp/loner distinction" hypothesis (Designer's Variant C rationale) requires per-NPC position data not emitted by the harness (Red-Teamer F3). The sweep is radius ∈ {4, 5, 6, 7} with a spatial-distribution metric (average NPC distance from player during fronts) to test whether smaller radius exposes more stragglers without reducing spawn count.

Self-burn immunity (0 self-burns across all overlap scenarios, P4). Front spawn suppression in do-nothing runs (dominance < 0.55 prevents all triggers, P3). Win/loss/none counts unchanged across variants (3/0/5 split held for all throughput arms). Median terminal sleep stable at 13–14 for winning seeds, 24 for do-nothing losses.

Per-tick straggler grey rate below step-cap saturation (no probe measures ticks-to-grey for an unheld NPC; Red-Teamer A2 claims it unchanged at 5 ticks for v=5 NPC, but this is arithmetic inference, not measurement). Spatial distribution of front coverage (radius-reduction effect on exposed stragglers requires position data not emitted, Red-Teamer F3). Late-game duty cycle at cooldown 2 baseline (the 0.65 figure is inferred from prior runs, not measured here; this run's 32-sleep cap truncates late-game observation). Campaign win rates (throughput bot hit the sleep cap in 5/8 seeds; terminal outcomes are "none," not wins or losses, so the 3/8 win count undercounts viable trajectories). The 8-seed sample size is small-n for median stability — a single-seed shift moves the median by 0.5 fronts (observed: A and C both 2.5, one more spawn in any seed pushes to 3.0).
