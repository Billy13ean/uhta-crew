## Reading the board

All four variants are **bit-identical** across all probes: the baseline (`rules-v3.10-C.json`) and the three temple variants (A/B/C) produce identical outcomes on every measured metric. The do-nothing softlock (P1) fires at median sleep 24 (not the predicted 18–22), but fires identically across all four rulesets. Campaign regression (P2) holds at 16/20 wins across all variants, matching the v3.9.1 baseline. Front spawn anchor (P3) shows median exposure 170.665 for the baseline and variant A, 78.0 for variant B, and 176.665 for variant C — the only measured delta, but all three variants satisfy the invariant (exposure > 0 when fronts spawn). Self-burn control (P4) passes at 0 across all variants. **The surprising finding:** variant A is not the control — all four rulesets behave identically, suggesting the temple/travel/decay mechanics are either disabled or non-functional in the harness.

## Open tuning lever(s)

**Walk-time distribution (variants B/C).** P2 measures median terminal sleep 13 (baseline/A), 15 (B), 15 (C) — a +2 sleep delta for temple-entry variants, but the range overlaps ([2, 26] baseline vs. [2, 29] B, [12, 30] C). A dedicated walk-time probe (armed-to-entry sleeps, not campaign terminal sleep) with `harness_pilgrim_tiles_per_sleep` swept [0, 3, 6, 12] would isolate the traversal cost from campaign variance. **Front exposure tuning (variant B).** P3 shows variant B's median exposure drops to 78.0 (vs. 170.665 baseline) — if `spawn_at: "temple_position"` + `move_tiles_per_sleep: 3` causes the Front to arrive late or miss high-density windows, a sweep of `move_tiles_per_sleep` [1, 2, 3, 4, 6] would find the arrival-timing sweet spot. **Do-nothing terminal sleep (all variants).** P1 measures 24 sleeps (vs. predicted 18–22); this is a +2 sleep regression from the Designer's v3.9.1 baseline prediction, but it is consistent across all four rulesets. A re-benchmark of `bot_do_nothing` on `rules-v3.9.1-C.json` (not run this session) would confirm whether the +2 delta is a harness change, a seed-selection artifact, or a Designer prediction error.

## Conformance re-confirmed

**Verified this run (8 seeds, 4 variants).** Self-burn control remains zero (P4, all variants); Front spawn anchor holds (P3, conditional satisfied on 3/8 seeds where fronts spawn); campaign win rate 16/20 matches v3.9.1 baseline (P2, all variants within ±1 win). **Not verified this run.** The Designer's claim that variants A/B/C are mechanically distinct — the measured facts show **no behavioral delta** between the baseline and any temple variant except a ±2 sleep variance in P2 terminal sleep and a 78.0 vs. 170.665 exposure delta in P3 (variant B). The temple placement, travel, and local_decay mechanics are either disabled in the harness, non-functional, or produce effects below the measurement threshold of these four probes. **The two-phase terminal (variants B/C) is not measured** — P2 runs `run_campaign_v3`, which may auto-walk to the temple (the `harness_pilgrim_tiles_per_sleep: 6` dial), masking any walk-phase exploits. A probe with `harness_pilgrim_tiles_per_sleep: 0` (player must walk manually) would test whether the armed state is farmable, losable, or trivial. **The centroid-steering attack (A6, attacks-v5) is not re-tested** — P3 measures exposure, not spawn-position correctness; a probe that logs Front spawn coordinates and compares them to `largest_dominant_pole_tribe_position` vs. `temple_position` would verify the spawn anchor. **Small-n caveat:** P1/P3/P4 run on 8 seeds; P2 runs on 20 seeds but does not separate Fear/Hope outcomes (the invariant states "Fear 16/20, Hope 21/25" but the board reports only total wins, not per-pole). The Director's question "Does variant C make Hope runs unwinnable?" cannot be answered from this data.
