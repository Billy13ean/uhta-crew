# The one who runs. Role-based so it plays in any era / as anyone.

ask the one who watches the ridge what it is, and don't wait for the answer
hide behind the others when the light rises
run from it
tell the child to be quiet about what they saw
promise the old one I'll stay, then back away toward the wall
run
go up to the ridge, to the red one, alone
wait
stand with whoever is still near me
confess what I have been keeping
wait for the dawn
