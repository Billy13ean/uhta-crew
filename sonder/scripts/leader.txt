# The one they look to. Role-based so it plays in any era / as anyone.
tell everyone we stay together tonight and mean it
give the child my own share of the water
walk toward the tall one so they can see someone do it
stand between the one who hums and the ridge
tell the old one I believe the story and ask him to tell it again, to all of us
share food with the one with the broken weapon
hand the child the thing I carry, for keeps
tend to anyone who has gone still
lead everyone out to the marsh, to the green one
wait with them
tell them the truth I have been keeping
wait for the dawn
