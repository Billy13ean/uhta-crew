The avatar & the flames

The unnamed being and the white flame that becomes what you do with it. The run's identity is written on the body: red veins in a Fear run, green vines in a Hope run (GDD v0.9.5). Era-invariant by ruling.

Era I — nomads (the world before anyone builds)

Hooded wanderers on a fogbound rockscape; belief state via ramp substitution: grey → tentative → devout, per pole.

Era II — villages

Tribes that crossed the threshold of feeling stopped wandering and built. Coifs and tunics; thatched cottages vs. spiked palisades.

Era III — Victorian towns

Top hats and long coats; row houses with warm windows vs. the dark mill and its smokestack. Clocktowers and smoking factories are the named furniture (GDD §2 visual canon).

The era-invariant (mythic constants)

Zealots, loners and the burned never age with the eras — timeless by Director ruling (v0.9.4). The burned wear a faint ring of the color they used to be.

Roads — the ground remembering you

Per-tile aging, decoupled from era art: compacted earth → cobble → paver stone; allegiance colors them green / red / grey.

Beacons & basins

Placed beacons in each color, and the unlit ruined basin from the opening myth — five discoverable on the map.

Verb & event language

The roar, the clash of rival spheres, conversion sparkles — every mechanic's render cue, because this game has no other channel.

Settings — composed scenes

Full scenes from the scene generators: the world mock, the cave opening's temple, and the title. These are the closest thing to screenshots the pipeline produces deterministically; in-game captures can be dropped into style/inspiration/ alongside them.

Palette audit (deterministic)

Code, not judgment: fraction of opaque pixels farther than tolerance 28 from the nearest locked color. Sprites authored from ramps should sit near zero; composed scenes carry glow and fog and are expected to drift — reported so drift is visible, not to fail it. Worst eight:
