uhta · the canon bible

Every rule the two Evaluators enforce, and the bench to rule on them. A rule is UPHELD (the default), AMENDED (your edited law is enforced instead; the baseline is kept as history), or REPEALED (not enforced — and the skip is logged in every run's CANON-IN-FORCE.md). There is deliberately no ignore button: a silent skip would make every green run log unfalsifiable. This page changes nothing by itself — it writes a ruling (canon/CANON-RULING.json), and the next run obeys it.

Encounter law — the mini-game pipeline

Encounter law — wordless, diegetic, pole-asymmetricmg-encounter-rules · proseUPHELD
source: GDD v0.9.9 §2 (Encounters) · enforced by: Designer/Judge/Refiner prompts ({{RULES}}); judge classes NOT-DIEGETIC and POLE-SYMMETRY cite it
“GDD §2, Encounters: when you meet a group directly the simulation pauses and you play a short, wordless, diegetic exchange — 'no interface, no text, only your body and theirs'. 'This is the only time you act on individuals rather than a population', and 'the two poles never play the same game': Hope plays patience, depth, few (converts arrive devout); Fear plays speed, force, many (converts are shallow). A design that needs a HUD, a label, or a word has failed; a design whose Hope and Fear versions are the same game reskinned has failed.”
not repealable — The Judge cannot judge against no law. Amend the text instead.

Tellings — where a Sonder story may surface in the wordless gamesonder-telling-surface · proseAMENDED
source: GDD v0.9.9 §1 Tone (wordless after the first dawn; ruled v0.9.2) + sonder/README §6 · enforced by: the slice: the Sonder hook (bank/sonder-teller.js) may be wired only under an AMENDED status of this rule; the telling text itself is gated at bank time (sonder/engine/style_gate.py: F1–F3, V1–V3, Z1, F4 for Register B)
“Baseline (the pillar as written): after the first dawn the game shows no text. A Sonder telling — the third-person retelling of a person's experience of the being, banked by the Sonder engine — has NO mid-run surface; candidates are banked only, for the endscreen epilogue or a Director ruling.”
AMENDED to: “A Sonder telling MAY be shown in play, under four scopes and no others. (1) WHEN: at a conversion — the tick an NPC enters the devout band under the player's colour (flame, beacon or road), or at the player's first contact with a settlement of the opposing colour; never on a tick where nothing changed for a person. (2) WHERE: as a paused card over the world, the simulation held while it is up, dismissed by any input; never as persistent overworld text, never on the HUD. (3) WHICH: drawn from bank/sonder-bank.js by Sonder.pick matched to the player's colour (flame:fear for a Fear run, flame:hope for a Hope run), preferring the settlement's era and a gate-clean telling; no telling repeats in a run until the pool is spent. (4) HOW OFTEN: once per conversion event, with a cooldown of at least one Sleep between cards, and never during the sleep-0 narrated cycle (the teacher's voice is alone there). The telling is shown as banked — third person, no digits, no exclamation, no interface words, never the word 'zealot' — and ends on its question, which is left unanswered on screen. Everything else about the pillar stands: outside these cards the game after the first dawn shows no text.”
Added 2026-08-21 when the Sonder engine landed. Amending it is the Director's sanction for the telling to appear in play; repealing it would mean tellings are unconstrained, which the pillar does not allow — prefer AMEND with scope.
