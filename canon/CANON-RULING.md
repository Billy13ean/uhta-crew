## sonder-telling-surface — Tellings — where a Sonder story may surface in the wordless game: **AMENDED**

Amended text: "A Sonder telling MAY be shown in play, under four scopes and no others. (1) WHEN: at a conversion — the tick an NPC enters the devout band under the player's colour (flame, beacon or road), or at the player's first contact with a settlement of the opposing colour; never on a tick where nothing changed for a person. (2) WHERE: as a paused card over the world, the simulation held while it is up, dismissed by any input; never as persistent overworld text, never on the HUD. (3) WHICH: drawn from bank/sonder-bank.js by Sonder.pick matched to the player's colour (flame:fear for a Fear run, flame:hope for a Hope run), preferring the settlement's era and a gate-clean telling; no telling repeats in a run until the pool is spent. (4) HOW OFTEN: once per conversion event, with a cooldown of at least one Sleep between cards, and never during the sleep-0 narrated cycle (the teacher's voice is alone there). The telling is shown as banked — third person, no digits, no exclamation, no interface words, never the word 'zealot' — and ends on its question, which is left unanswered on screen. Everything else about the pillar stands: outside these cards the game after the first dawn shows no text."

Reason: The Director's intent (2026-08-21): when the being converts someone, the player should hear what that was like for a person who lived it — a fear story for a fear run. Class: CONTRADICTS-LOCKED against §1 Tone (wordless after the first dawn). Ruling: AMEND, scoped to the conversion moment as a paused card, so the overworld stays wordless and the card is an encounter-class surface, the same class the 2026-08-19 presentation amendment proposes for encounters. Transcribed to GDD v0.9.10 §1 and §2.
